# Glassfort trademark policy

The name **Glassfort** and the **Glassfort logo** ("the marks") identify the
official Glassfort distribution. They are **not** covered by the licences of
this repository: code is licensed under GPL-3.0-or-later (`LICENSE`), and
documentation and artwork under CC BY-SA 4.0 (`LICENSES/CC-BY-SA-4.0.txt`).
Those licences give you the right to use, change and share the work, but not
the right to use the marks.

The purpose of this policy is simple: when someone sees the name Glassfort,
they should be able to trust that it is the real thing, built and signed by
the Glassfort project.

The marks are currently unregistered. This policy applies regardless of
registration status.

## Allowed without asking

- **Talking about Glassfort:** news articles, reviews, blog posts, videos,
  tutorials, books, conference talks and academic work.
- **Unmodified redistribution:** mirroring or passing on official Glassfort
  ISO images and packages exactly as published, with their checksums and
  signatures intact.
- **Saying what your work is compatible with:** for example "packages for
  Glassfort" or "a theme for Glassfort", as long as it is clear that your work
  is not made or endorsed by the Glassfort project.
- **Community use:** user groups, meetups, stickers and similar
  non-commercial material that promotes Glassfort, as long as it does not
  suggest it is official.

## Not allowed without written permission

- **Modified builds under the Glassfort name.** If you change packages, the
  package selection, default configuration or signing keys and distribute the
  result, you must give it a different name and remove the Glassfort logo and
  branding packages (`glassfort-branding`). "Based on Glassfort" in its
  description is fine.
- **Implying endorsement.** Do not suggest that the Glassfort project
  sponsors, certifies or supports your product, service, company or event.
- **Confusingly similar marks.** Do not use names, logos or domain names that
  could be confused with Glassfort.
- **Commercial merchandise** that uses the marks.
- **Changing the logo.** Use the logo only in its official form, colours and
  proportions.

## Other projects' marks

Glassfort is based on Arch Linux but is not affiliated with or endorsed by
the Arch Linux project. Glassfort does not use Arch Linux's name or logo as
part of its branding. Apple, macOS, Microsoft and Windows are trademarks of
their respective owners; Glassfort uses none of their assets.

## Questions and permission requests

Open an issue in the Glassfort GitHub repository or contact the maintainer
through GitHub. If you are unsure whether your use is allowed, ask – we are
happy to help.
